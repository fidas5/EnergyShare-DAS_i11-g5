
Solar Energy and Household Consumption

Solar photovoltaic panels convert sunlight
into electrical energy.

A household may produce more electricity than
it consumes during certain periods.

Energy surplus can be estimated as:
Surplus (kWh) = Production (kWh) - Consumption (kWh)

When the result is negative, there is no positive
surplus available.

EnergyShare records household energy readings and
calculates declared surplus values.

These readings are provided by users in the initial
prototype rather than collected from physical
smart meters.

Energy efficiency suggestions include shifting
flexible electricity usage toward periods of
higher solar production.
