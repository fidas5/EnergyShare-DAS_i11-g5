# EnergyShare Energy Marketplace

The Energy Marketplace Service is responsible
for publishing solar energy surplus offers
and managing consumer requests.

Producers publish offers containing the available
energy quantity in kWh, price per kWh, location
zone, and availability period.

Consumers can browse available offers and
request an energy reservation.

The marketplace validates available surplus
through the Energy Monitoring Service using
synchronous OpenFeign communication.

# EnergyShare Project Overview

EnergyShare is a prototype web platform designed
for Tunisian households that produce or consume
solar energy.

The project is motivated by electricity supply
challenges, including summer power interruptions
in Tunisia.

Households can record solar production and
electricity consumption, calculate energy surplus,
and participate in simulated energy exchanges.

EnergyShare does not physically transfer electricity
or process real payments.

Solar Energy and Household Consumption

Solar photovoltaic panels convert sunlight
into electrical energy.

A household may produce more electricity than
it consumes during certain periods.

Energy surplus can be estimated as:
Surplus (kWh) = Production (kWh) - Consumption (kWh)

When the result is negative, there is no positive
surplus available.

EnergyShare records household energy readings and
calculates declared surplus values.
